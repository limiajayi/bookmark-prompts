# Privacy Policy for Bookmark Propmts

**Last updated:** 24/10/2026

Prompts Bookmarks is a browser extension for Chrome and Firefox that lets you bookmark prompts and responses on claude.ai and save them for later. This policy explains what the extension stores, where it stores it, and what it doesn't do.

## The short version

- Your bookmarks stay on your device.
- Nothing is sent to me or to anyone else.
- I have no servers, accounts, analytics, ads, or tracking.

## What the extension stores

When you bookmark something, the extension saves:

- The prompt text
- The response text
- The chat URL
- The chat ID
- A timestamp
- A unique bookmark ID

## Where it is stored

Bookmarks are saved in your browser's extension storage (`storage.local`). This storage is separate for each browser profile, so bookmarks saved in one profile or browser won't appear in another. The data is stored on your device and is not encrypted by the extension.

## What is not collected

The extension does not collect, transmit, or sell any data. It makes no network requests to send your data anywhere, and it has no servers, accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind. I cannot see or access your bookmarks.

## Secret detection

Before saving, the extension scans the text and warns you if it looks like it contains an API key or password. This check runs entirely on your device.

This check is a best-effort guess, not a guarantee. It can miss secrets, and it can also flag things that aren't secrets. Please don't rely on it to keep sensitive data safe. If something is sensitive, don't bookmark it.

## Viewing and deleting your data

You can view, copy, and delete your bookmarks at any time from inside the extension.

If you uninstall the extension, your browser removes its storage, including all saved bookmarks. Since the data only exists on your device, uninstalling is a complete deletion.

## Not affiliated with Anthropic

This extension is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Anthropic. "Claude" is mentioned only to describe the website the extension works with.
